# D04 — Mentors are reached through their own link; a public list is opt-in
- Date, owner: 2026-10-21, Łukasz Lasota (ran the om-discover session); chosen from three presented options, no agent recommendation given
- Context and the options weighed: link and opt-in list; link only (Topmate pattern); searchable catalogue of all mentors. The staff engineer refused to be listed next to strangers and wants to send their own link; developers want to find someone in minutes; 21 of 50 candidate mentors have no warm contact yet.
- Decision and why: every mentor gets a shareable page link; a mentor chooses whether to appear on a simple public list.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: mentee-side discovery depends on mentors' own audiences, which are unknown for 23 of 50 candidates. Revisit if bookings come mostly from the list, or if mentors with no audience get no bookings.
