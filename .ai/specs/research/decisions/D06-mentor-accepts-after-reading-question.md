# D06 — A booking needs a written question; the mentor accepts or declines it, and a decline refunds in full
- Date, owner: 2026-10-21, Łukasz Lasota (ran the om-discover session); accepted the agent's recommendation
- Context and the options weighed: mentor acceptance after reading the question; instant booking of an open slot with the question shown before the session. Two mentors (2026-09-03) want the question first and refuse "homework"; mentees want speed (2026-08-20, 2026-09-01).
- Decision and why: the question is required to book; the mentor accepts or declines; a decline returns the full prepayment automatically.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: acceptance adds waiting before the session, which conflicts with the urgent case (see D05). Revisit if measured acceptance delay makes mentees abandon bookings.
