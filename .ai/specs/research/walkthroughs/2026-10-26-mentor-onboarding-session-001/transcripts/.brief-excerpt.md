# DevMentor — what the product is (excerpt for the walk)
## Product and how it stands out

- What it is: a marketplace of invited senior engineers where a developer books and prepays a 25- or 50-minute text session (Scope). 
- What makes it different: text instead of video, public work instead of ratings, and a mentor found through a stack-tag list or their own share link.


## Scope

- **Now (1.0):** accounts with GitHub sign-in (R11); invited mentors with a mentor page, public work and stack tags, prices within bounds and published slots (R04, R12, R13); finding a mentor through the stack-tag list or a share link (R08); booking a slot at least two hours ahead and paying for a 25/50-minute session through Stripe Checkout (R02, R09, R06, R14); the text session and the written answer (R01); free cancellation up to 24 hours before (R10); a note the mentor drafts and the mentee approves, kept privately (R15, R03); the two founders as the operator (R16). 
- **First iteration:** 25 hours is the budget of the first iteration, not of all of 1.0; it must leave a bookable, payable, answerable session. Which stories fit is for the backlog; the reference brief's 14-story list was not adopted. 
- **Later:** publishing notes into a vault, vault subscriptions, MCP access, async questions with a 24-hour reply, team plans; no vault work before the D22 readout. 
- **Not doing:** see Non-goals.

## Domain glossary

| Term | Meaning | Owned by | Visible to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session | a 25- or 50-minute text exchange about one question; its length is not the waiting time before it starts | mentor and mentee | both |
| Slot | a start time the mentor publishes; bookable only when it starts at least two hours later | mentor | mentees |
| Mentor page | a mentor's shareable page with public work, stack tags, price and open slots | mentor | anyone with the link |
| Mentor list | all mentors filtered by stack tag, ordered by most recent published availability, never by score | platform | everyone |
| Session note | the mentor's write-up after a session; the mentee approves or declines it; kept privately by both | mentor | mentor, mentee |
| Operator | the two founders, doing by hand what has no story yet | founders | internal |
| Vault | Later: a mentor's notes sold as a subscription | mentor | subscribers |

## Key flows

- Future state, "Mentor onboarding (1.0)": the operator invites a beachhead engineer in a batch of 20, asking for one bookable session within two weeks (R13); the mentor signs in with GitHub (R11), fills the page with public work and stack tags (R04), sets 25/50-minute prices within bounds (R12), publishes slots, shares the link, and completes Stripe Connect before the first payout (R14). 

## Business rules (active)
 R01 | Sessions are text; no video calls; the product says so on every session screen 
 R02 | A session is 25 or 50 minutes 
 R03 | Nothing from a session is published without the mentee's approval 
 R04 | Every mentor page carries a link to public work and a plain description of what they have done; no scores 
 R06 | Payments go through Stripe only, paid before the session 
 R08 | A mentee finds a mentor through the list filtered by stack tag (TypeScript, React, Python, AI agents) or the mentor's share link; ordered by most recent published availability; no search, no ranking 
 R09 | A slot can be booked only when it starts at least two hours after booking; no promise of speed; the time from booking to start is recorded for every booking 
 R10 | Free cancellation up to 24 hours before a session; later cancellations forfeit the fee; disputes resolved by the operator; no automatic refunds in 1.0 
 R11 | Sign-in with GitHub for every role; email and password as the fallback 
 R12 | Each mentor sets their own 25- and 50-minute prices within operator-set bounds; the platform fee is 20% of a session, configurable by the operator 
 R13 | Mentors join by invitation only, in batches of 20 from the beachhead pool; each invitation asks for one bookable session within two weeks 
 R14 | Stripe Checkout for sessions, Stripe Connect for payouts; a mentor completes Connect onboarding before the first payout 
 R15 | The mentor drafts the session note; the mentee approves it in full or declines it with a comment; the mentor redrafts; no co-editing 
 R16 | An operator action without a story in the current iteration is done by hand by the founders and logged in a shared note 

## Non-goals
- N01 video calls; N02 ratings or a reviews profile
