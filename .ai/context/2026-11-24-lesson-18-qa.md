# Lesson 18 — tester's verdict on #79 (om-qa-buddy, AI and human in parallel)

## Evidence card

- **Requirement / hypothesis:** #79 — the session length under each open time on the mentor page did not look like something you can choose. After the fix, a mentee sees a "Length" caption and one dot per option, recognises the choice, and can switch it by mouse and keyboard; payment opens at the chosen length's price.
- **Scope and risk:** `SlotBookingControls` (mentor page `/m/<slug>`, booking start). Risk low: presentation of an existing radio group; booking and payment logic unchanged. Business risk: a mentee who cannot see the choice books 25 minutes when 50 was wanted.
- **Base and target commit:** base `origin/main` 07020cf; fix 0592fbe on `fix/issue-79-session-length`, PR #97.
- **Environment, seed, account and time:** local test env (`sh .ai/scripts/test-env-up.sh`), data from `node .ai/scripts/qa-setup-length-choice.mjs` (one time with 25 + 50, one time where only 25 fits). Accounts: visitor, `mock-mentee`, `mock-mentor`, `mock-operator` (demo accounts). AI run 2026-11-23 (headless Chromium, `agent-browser`); human run 2026-11-24 (Chrome on Windows, after a machine restart, new port and new times).
- **Check:** test plan TC-079-001..008; AI: `node .ai/scripts/qa-length-choice-walk.mjs`; human: the runbook published before the AI clicked anything; owner: `npm run test:integration -- booking-checkout`.
- **Expected:** caption and dots visible, 25 chosen by default, 50 by click and arrow keys, payment at PLN 200.00 for 50, one chosen option when only 25 fits, hover and pointer, no horizontal scroll at 375 px, readable in dark mode, options locked while payment opens.
- **Actual result and evidence:** AI 9/10 checks pass, hover inconclusive (headless does not apply `:hover`); human confirmed 001 and 004 on screenshots and passed 005 (hover), 007 (dark mode, forced from the console) and 008 (lock, network throttled to Slow 3G; tester: "probably OK"). Integration 13/13. Report and screenshots: `.ai/qa/artifacts_qa-buddy-issue-79/` (local, gitignored); knowledge base: `.ai/qa/knowledge-base/`.
- **What this does not prove:** real users recognising the choice (no user test); dark mode as users would get it (the app has no theme switch); the lock on a real slow network (only simulated); Safari and Firefox (not run).
- **Decision and owner:** ✅ GO — the project owner as tester, 2026-11-24. The AI draft was ⚠️ CONDITIONAL GO until the three human-only cases were checked.

## AI vs human

| | AI | Human |
|---|---|---|
| Found | BUG-001 (price chips in "Sessions" look like length options), FR-001 (accessible name of each group uses the UTC time) | no new defect |
| Could judge | structure, selection by click and keys, price on payment, narrow screen | hover, dark mode, the short "Opening payment…" lock |
| Could not judge | hover (headless), what "looks clickable" means to a person, sub-second states | the code-level cause; repeatable runs |

The useful split: the AI covered every scripted case and two defects outside the change; the human closed the visual and timing cases and owned the verdict. Two cases (007, 008) needed a trick to be testable at all, which is now in `.ai/qa/knowledge-base/risk-hotspots.md`.
