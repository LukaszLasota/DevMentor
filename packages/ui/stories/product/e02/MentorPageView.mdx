# Mentor page view

The body of the share page `/m/[slug]` (specs #23, #28) and the preview on `/mentor/profile`. Same for every visitor; the owner sees no edit link.

## Section order (spec #28)
1. Header: `h1` display name, avatar (`alt=""`), stack tags.
2. "What I have done" (bio as plain text, in full) and "Public work" (new tab, `rel="noopener nofollow ugc"`).
3. "Sessions": always "Sessions are text — no video call. 25 or 50 minutes." (N01). If the offer is ready: price chips, "Times are shown in your time zone", the open times; a slot past its lead time stays visible but is marked "Starts in less than two hours — no longer bookable". If not: only "Not bookable yet" — no prices, no times, no reason.

## Rules
| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| No booking button until E03 (#29) | spec #28 |
| No ratings or counts | N02 |
| Disabled slot: `aria-disabled`, disabled text colour and a written reason, not colour alone | WCAG 1.4.1 |
| Times via `LocalTime` | spec #22 |
