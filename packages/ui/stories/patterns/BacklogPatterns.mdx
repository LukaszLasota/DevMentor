## Remove from your own list (*proposal*, from the agent test)
For personal lists such as favorites: no confirm dialog. The row disappears; a status line above the list says what happened and offers Undo (`Button appearance="text" size="sm"`); focus moves to the next row's action, or to the list heading when the list is now empty. A failed removal shows its error under the row with Try again. Rows: name as the link to the profile, secondary line muted, one row action = `text` button, `sm`.
