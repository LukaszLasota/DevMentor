# Card

## 1. Purpose
Groups one self-contained piece of a screen: an onboarding step, the invitation, a sign-in panel.

## 2. When to use
`/mentor` onboarding steps, `/invitation/[token]`, `/sign-in` and `/register` panels (specs #21, #22).

## 3. When not to use
- Rows of a list: rows are separated by thin lines, not cards (R01.1).
- Inside another card (A01).
- To make a section look important: use a heading and spacing.

## 4. Anatomy
Container (1 px `--border`, `--dm-radius-card`, `p-panel`) · header (title, description, optional action) · content · footer separated by a line.

## 5. Variants
One look. No shadow, no fill colour (R03.2).

<Canvas of={Stories.ContentOnly} />

## 6. States
Static. A card that is a link target gets a focus outline on the link inside it, not on the card.

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility
`CardTitle` is a `div`: wrap the title in the right heading level for the page (`<CardTitle><h2>…</h2></CardTitle>`) so the outline stays correct.

## 8. Content
Title is a task or noun; description is one sentence about what to do.

## 12. Do and don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Footer: Cancel first, then the primary action (R03.4) | Primary action on the left in one card and the right in another |
| Thin border, no shadow | Shadow as the only separation (A01) |

## 13. Related
