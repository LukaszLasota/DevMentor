# E02 Finding a mentor

A developer finds a mentor through the stack-tag list or a mentor's own share link, sees what they have built, their prices and open times.

## Flow
1. `/mentors` — filter by one stack tag; mentors with the most recently published open times first; no search, no ranking (spec #27).
2. `/m/[slug]` — the share page: who, what they have done, public work, sessions (spec #28). Booking arrives with E03.
